Nairobi creates over 3,000 tonnes of garbage every single day, but less than half gets collected
because picking up small KES 250 trash bags house-by-house across crowded estates just doesn't make enough money for truck
owners. Our smart platform fixes this dirty crisis by connecting garbage trucks to big, urgent jobs like hauling construction
waste or septic tanks, while charging private waste companies KES 3,500 a month per truck to manage their routes and
automatically collect monthly customer payments through M-Pesa. By filling up extra space in moving trucks, using GPS
tracking to stop drivers from illegally dumping waste in local rivers or vacant plots, and giving Nairobi families
instant M-Pesa cashback for separating plastic recyclables at home, we make waste collection clean, profitable, and
fair for everyone.

Why this matters
