Nairobi Waste Action

Across high-density Nairobi estates (such as Eastlands, Kibera, Mathare, Mukuru, and Kasarani), secondary day-school students commuting on foot encounter pervasive illegal open dumpsites and waste-clogged drainage culverts. Lacking actionable channels for waste recovery, residents routinely burn trash, exposing students to toxic PM2.5 particulates or dumping plastic waste directly into open storm drains, severely polluting pedestrian corridors and triggering flash flooding.

Why this matters

Nairobi generates over 2,400 tons of solid waste daily, leaving more than 1,300 tons uncollected in illegal dumpsites and waterways.
